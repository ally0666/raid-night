# Raid Night

Soft reserves for TBC raids, in game. Same loot list as the Raid Night website: Karazhan, Gruul, Magtheridon, SSC, The Eye, Hyjal, Black Temple, Zul'Aman, and Sunwell.

## What it does

- Pick 1–10 items per raid (lead sets the count with `/rn limit 2`)
- Filter by boss, search by name, hide gear your class cannot use
- Share picks with the party or raid automatically
- Export a Gargul CSV (`/rn export`, then `/gl sr`)

## How to use

Type `/rn` after you join the raid. Choose the instance, click your items. Everyone else running Raid Night sees those picks.

## Notes

This addon does not log in to the website and does not replace Discord. It is the loot sheet, in WoW.

Requires Burning Crusade Classic / TBC Anniversary (interface 20506).
