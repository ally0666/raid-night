# Raid Night changelog

## 1.6.10
- The in-game walkthrough covers everything new: the Discord invite, Trash and Recipes, Submit reserves and the minimap button. Anyone who finished the old walkthrough sees the new one once.

## 1.6.9
- New minimap button. Left-click opens Raid Night, right-click opens the raid leader settings, drag to move it. /rn minimap hides or shows it.
- New Submit reserves button on the Picks tab. It confirms your picks reached the raid leader and turns green when you're done.
- Clearer messages while picking ("1 of 2 picked", "All picked").

## 1.6.7
- Added trash drops, recipes, epic gems and boss quest items for every raid. Find them under Trash and Recipes in the boss dropdown.
- Fixed items being hidden from classes that can use them: cloaks, rings, necks, trinkets, ranged weapons and off-armor pieces now show for every class that can equip and use them.

## 1.6.6
- Fixed: the raid sheet no longer pops up in normal parties. It only goes out after the leader clicks Start sheet.
- New: the raid leader can add a Discord invite on the Raid tab (or /rn discord discord.gg/yourcode). Raiders see it in the addon and in raid chat, and can copy it.
- The item list is easier to read: taller rows, bigger icons, more space between lines.
- Tidied the Raid tab so no text runs outside the window.
